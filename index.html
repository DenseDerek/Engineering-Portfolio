01

What I worked on

  • Learned to set up and independently operate a robotic press brake.
  • Built confidence running CNC punch-press work with close attention to tooling and measurements.
  • Read engineering drawings to prepare and complete fabrication tasks with limited supervision.
  • Supported countersinking, tapping, PEM installation, surface finishing, and powder-coating preparation.
02

What the work taught me

Not every run went perfectly. This warped sheet-metal part became one of the most useful lessons from my internship. I learned to slow down, confirm the tooling, double-check the drawing, triple-check the measurements, and ask a mentor for a final look before starting a run.
